This video is based on windows shortcuts related to typing.

 "ctrl" + "s"  : to save the document .
 "ctrl" + "a"  : to select all lines in document
 "ctrl" + "a" + "delete" or "backspace"  : to delete all lines in document
 "ctrl" + "z"  : use to get removed text again .
 "ctrl" + "c"  : to copy the text .
 "ctrl" + "v"  : to paste the text .
 "shift" + "home" : to select whole line .
